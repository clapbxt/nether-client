# In game

The Nether client runs inside every profile that uses one of the **Nether** ways to play (see [Profiles](profiles.md)).
It adds a menu, a set of optional **modules** (HUD, visuals and utilities), its own title screen, and your friends and
party.

## The Nether menu

Press **Right Shift** in game or on the title screen. A small hub opens with four buttons:

| Button | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Mods** | The module list: turn modules on and off and change their settings |
| **Wardrobe** | Your capes and cosmetics, with a live preview |
| **Friends** | Your friends, invites and party |
| **Settings** | The client's own settings (look, controls, menu key) |

The same buttons sit on the Nether title screen, so you can use them before you join a world.

### Finding a module

The **Mods** page sorts modules into tabs: **Favorites**, **All**, **HUD**, **Visual**, **PvP** and **Utility**.
Press **Ctrl+F** to search by name. Star a module to add it to Favorites.

Click a module to turn it on or off. Open its settings to change colours (with a colour picker, including a rainbow
"chroma" option), sizes, keys and other options. Every setting has a short explanation; slider values can also be typed
in.

The full list is on the [Module list](modules.md) page.

### Moving HUD elements

Open the **HUD Editor** tab (or press **Ctrl+E** in the menu) and drag HUD boxes where you want them. Boxes snap to
corners and edges of the screen and keep their place when you resize the window or change the GUI scale. Boxes you
never move stack neatly in their corner.

### Keyboard control

The menu works without a mouse:

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| **Arrows** / **Tab** | Move between items |
| **Enter** / **Space** | Use the selected item |
| **Left** / **Right** | Change the selected setting |
| **Esc** | Go back one step |
| **Ctrl+F** | Search |
| **Ctrl+E** | HUD Editor |

You can change the key that opens the menu in the client settings.

## The title screen

The Nether title screen replaces the game's own. It shows a background, your account, quick join for your recent
servers, and your **party**: you and your friends standing together in your own skins and capes. See
[Friends, parties and world invites](friends.md).

## Where your settings are kept

Changes you make in game are saved as you go, for the profile you are playing. A new profile starts from your general
settings. The menu's look (Dark or Light, accent colour) follows the launcher's **Settings → Personalization**, so it is
the same everywhere.

## Playing fair

The Nether client only shows things the game already knows. It never plays for you, never sends anything to the
server, and never patches how the game draws the world. A few modules (such as Zoom, Fullbright and Reach Display) are
not allowed on some servers. Check the server's rules. Details for every module: [Fair play](../../SAFETY.md).
